## 3. LOAI THUE DOC DAO CUA VIET NAM

### 3.1 He thong thue phuc tap

| Loai thue | Mo ta | Do phuc tap | Co chatbot ho tro? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thue khoan** | Thu theo ty le % tren doanh thu (1-5%), phu thuoc nganh nghe, dia phuong | Roi rac, khong ai hieu ro | KHONG |
| **TNCN (01-TNCN)** | Ke khai khau tru 10%, co 7 muc giam tru gia canh | Phuc tap voi 7 muc, nam thang | KHONG |
| **VAT** | 0%, 5%, 8%, 10%, khau tru GTGT | Nhieu muc, phuc tap | KHONG |
| **TTDB (TTDBB)** | Thue tieu dung dac biet - bia, ruou, xang dau, dieu hoa | Ty le 10-150%, nhieu nganh | KHONG |
| **CIT** | Thue TNDN 20%, uu dai theo khoang, ozone | Phuc tap voi uu dai | KHONG |
| **TNCN 5% cho khoan** | Khau tru 5% khi chi tien cho ca nhan | nhieu truong hop | KHONG |
| **Phi bao hiem** | BHXH, BHTN, BNN 32.5% (DN 21.5%) | Bien dong theo luat moi | KHONG |

### 3.2 Van de thuong gap cua nguoi dung

**Ho kinh doanh ca the (5 trieu ho):**
- Khong hieu thue khoan tinh the nao
- Khong biet phai dong bao nhieu moi thang/quy
- So ngay quy ke khai khong ro rang
- Lo lang bi phat vi ke khai muon

**DN nho (SME - 600K DN):**
- Ke toan vien moi vao nghe khong nam ro quy trinh
- Thay doi luat thuong xuyen -> khong the cap nhat kip
- Khong biet cach ap dung uu dai thue
- Tong hop thue phuc tap (01-TNCN, 02-TNCN, 03-TNCN)

**Nhan vien cong ty (5 trieu nguoi):**
- Khong hieu cach giam tru gia canh TNCN
- Khong biet cach khai BHXH dung
- So lot thue thang, khong kiem tra duoc

Chinh phu dang day chuyen doi so nhung **CHUA co AI chatbot ho tro nguoi dan hoi thue**. Cac cong ty phan mem tap trung vao ke khai/thanh toan, khong ai lam "tro ly thue AI" tra loi cau hoi.

## 6. KHOANG TRONG & CO HOI CHO KETOANTHUETADA.COM

### 6.1 Gap #1 - KHONG CO CHATBOT THUE AI TAI VIET NAM

**Hien trang:** Khong co san pham nao o Viet Nam cung cap chatbot AI tra loi cau hoi ve thue theo ngon ngu thuong mai Viet Nam.

**Co hoi:** Tro thanh nguoi dau tien. 5 trieu ho kinh doanh + 600K DN nho + 5 trieu nhan vien can hoi thue = **16 trieu potential users**.

**Kha thi:** TADA da co 281 notes vault thue/ke toan + BM25 search + OpenRouter LLM. Du de tao MVP chatbot tra loi thue.

### 6.2 Gap #2 - THUE KHOAN - Bai toan khong ai giai quyet

**Hien trang:** Thue khoan la loai thue phuc tap nhat Viet Nam. Khong co phan mem nao ho tro tinh toan thue khoan dua tren nganh nghe va dia phuong.

**Co hoi:** Tinh thue khoan theo nganh nghe (1-5%), dia phuong, doanh thu. Ung dung di dong don gian nhap doanh thu -> tinh thue -> ke khai.

**Kha thi:** Du lieu ty le thue khoan da co trong vault (281 notes). Co the tich hop tinh toan.

### 6.3 Gap #3 - TNCN - 5 TRIEU NGUOI CAN GIUP

**Hien trang:** Nhan vien khong hieu cach giam tru gia canh. Co 7 loai giam tru, nhieu nguoi khong thuc day du.

**Co hoi:** Chatbot giup nhan vien tinh TNCN: nhap luong -> tinh giam tru -> cho biet so thue phai dong. Ung dung di dong cho nhan vien.

**Kha thi:** Cong thuc TNCN on dinh (NQ 54/2024/QH15), co the tich hop tinh toan.

### 6.4 Gap #4 - VAT - Lien tuc thay doi

**Hien trang:** Muc VAT thay doi theo thoi gian (5%, 8%, 10%). DN nho khong the cap nhat kip.

**Co hoi:** Chatbot cap nhat muc VAT moi nhat + tinh toan VAT theo hoa don. Ung dung tra cuu muc VAT theo loai hang hoa.

### 6.5 Gap #5 - SEO CONTENT - CHUA AI UNG DUNG VAO THUE

**Hien trang:** Hau het blog thue Viet Nam la noi dung tinh (blog), khong co ung dung tinh toan, khong co chatbot tra loi.

**Co hoi:** ketoanthuetada.com da co 51+ bai SEO + chatbox. Can them:
- Tinh toan thue khoan online (calculator)
- Tinh toan TNCN online (calculator)
- Chatbot tra loi thue 24/7
- Ung dung di dong

### 6.6 Gap #6 - CHINH SACH THUE THAY DOI NHANH

**Hien trang:** Viet Nam thay doi luat thue hang nam. Khong co cong cu nao tu dong cap nhat va thong bao cho nguoi dung.

**Co hoi:** Chatbot tu dong cap nhat khi co thay doi luat + thong bao cho nguoi dung. "Mot nam truoc, ban da hoi ve thue X. Hien tai luat da thay doi: Y."
